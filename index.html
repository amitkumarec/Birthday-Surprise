Happy Birthday.

An Apology from my end

I've been wanting to say this for a long time, and no matter how many times I try to put it into words, it never feels enough.

I'm sorry.

Not just for the message I sent that day, but for the anger, the hurtful words, and the way I chose to handle everything. Looking back, I can see how much anger and frustration had built up inside me, but that still doesn't justify what I said or did.

The truth is, instead of calmly explaining what I was feeling, I let my emotions take control. I turned my frustration into anger, and then I directed that anger at you.

The message I sent you was cruel. I said things that I don't truly believe, things I should never have said to someone I loved. Calling you names, attacking your character, saying that you were dead to me, and blocking you immediately afterward were immature and deeply unfair. You deserved better from me, even in the middle of a fight.

What hurts me the most now is remembering the sketch.

I had spent weeks making it. After long days and exhaustion, I would still sit down and work on it because it was meant for you. It wasn't just a drawing. It carried my time, effort, affection, and the happiness I felt imagining your reaction. In a moment of anger, I tore it apart and burned it.

At the time, I thought I was expressing my frustration. Now I realize I was destroying something beautiful because I couldn't control my emotions. The sketch deserved better, and so did you.

I also want you to know something that I failed to explain back then. I hadn't ignored Valentine's Day because I didn't care. I had actually been thinking about gifts and ways to make the day special. When you mentioned you were staying at your sister's place, I wasn't sure how to surprise you or where to send anything. Instead of talking openly about that confusion and misunderstanding, I became defensive and resentful.

But even if I had valid feelings, I handled them in the worst possible way.
I wish I had paused. I wish I had listened. I wish I had chosen understanding over anger.

Maybe we both misunderstood each other, but I can only take responsibility for my part, and my part was ugly. For that, I am genuinely sorry.

I'm not writing this letter to force forgiveness, erase the past, or make you feel guilty. I simply don't want the last memory of me to be that version of myself. You knew me at my best, but unfortunately you also saw me at my worst, and I deeply regret that.

Despite everything that happened, I am grateful for the moments we shared, the conversations, the laughter, the memories, and all the ways you became an important part of my life.

You deserved kindness from me that day, and instead you got anger.

For that, I am truly sorry.

I hope life has been kind to you. I hope you've found happiness, peace, and all the beautiful things you always deserved.

Take care.
